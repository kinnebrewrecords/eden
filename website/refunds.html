Cancellation and Refund Policy

Free trial

New subscriptions include the trial shown at checkout. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first charge.

Cancellation

You may cancel at any time through the Stripe billing portal on Eden’s account page. Your access continues through the end of the paid billing period, after which the subscription will not renew.

Refunds

Subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a paid billing period begins, except where required by law. If you believe you were charged because of a billing error, contact support within seven days of the charge and include the account email and Stripe receipt number.

Workspace export

Before access ends, download a current Eden workspace backup from Account & Cloud. Recovery access may remain available, but you should not depend on indefinite storage after cancellation.

Contact

Billing questions may be sent to eden.intelligence.support@gmail.com.
