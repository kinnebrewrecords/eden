Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Eden handles information when you visit the website, create an account, subscribe, or use the application.

Information we process

We process account details such as your email address; subscription status and Stripe customer identifiers; company profile information; and workspace content such as projects, estimates, pricing, schedules, customer details, and daily logs. Stripe processes payment-card details; Eden does not store complete card numbers.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, provide and secure private workspaces, calculate and generate requested outputs, administer subscriptions, provide support, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

Storage and service providers

Eden uses service providers including Supabase for authentication and cloud workspace storage, Stripe for billing, Streamlit for application hosting, Cloudflare for website delivery, and OpenAI when you use AI-assisted features. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to operate Eden, when you direct us to, or when legally required.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and workspace information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, legal, and recovery purposes. Contact support to request account deletion. Export your workspace before requesting deletion if you need a copy.

Security

Eden uses account authentication, encrypted network connections, and account-level database access controls. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password and notify support if you suspect unauthorized access.

Your choices

You may access and export workspace information from Eden, update account information, manage billing through Stripe, or request correction or deletion by contacting support.

Contact

Privacy requests may be sent to eden.intelligence.support@gmail.com.
